# How to lose a Guy in 10 Days
### Andie Anderson, a women’s magazine columnist, wants to write about serious topics but gets stuck with an assignment: prove how a woman can drive a guy away in 10 days by making every classic dating mistake. At the same time, Ben Barry, an ad executive, bets his coworkers he can make any woman fall in love with him in 10 days to win a major diamond account. They meet at a bar, and each unknowingly picks the other as their target. Andie smothers Ben with clinginess, pet names, and meddling, while Ben endures it all to win the bet. But as the days pass, they start to fall for each other for real. When both secrets come out at the worst possible moment, they split, and each has to decide whether the feelings are real enough to fight for. In the end, they reconcile, and Andie even gets to write the serious article she wanted.
